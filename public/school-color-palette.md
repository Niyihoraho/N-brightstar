# School Website Color Palette

A color system derived from the school's own uniforms — the house-color polos (red, green, blue, yellow) and the formal white shirt with red tartan. Built to feel warm and playful enough for young children, while staying clean and credible for parents.

---

## Primary colors

### Primary Blue
`#1D5FA5`

Sourced from the uniform shorts, worn by every student regardless of house color — the most consistent, unifying color across the whole school. This is the anchor color for the site.

**Use for:** header/navigation bar, primary buttons, links, footer background

### Off-White
`#FFFFFF`

Sourced from the formal shirt.

**Use for:** page background, content cards, negative space

### Charcoal Ink
`#2B2B2B`

Sourced from the shoes.

**Use for:** body text, footer text — avoid pure black (`#000000`), it feels harsher on screen

---

## Accent colors

### Sunshine Yellow
`#F5B800`

Sourced from the yellow house polo.

**Use for:** hover states, small icons, highlights, call-to-action badges, decorative touches (waves, dividers)

### Leaf Green
`#3E9B4F`

Sourced from the green house polo.

**Use for:** secondary buttons (e.g. "Enrol now"), success messages, section background tints

### Cherry Red
`#D8342F`

Sourced from the red house polo and the tartan uniform.

**Use for:** small badges, alerts, sports/house references — use sparingly, large red blocks can read as a warning online

---

## Suggested color balance (60 / 30 / 10 rule)

| Share | Color | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 55% | Off-White `#FFFFFF` | Background, breathing room |
| 25% | Primary Blue `#1D5FA5` | Structure — nav, headers, buttons |
| 12% | Sunshine Yellow `#F5B800` | Warmth — accents, hover, highlights |
| 8% | Leaf Green / Cherry Red | Small accents only — badges, tags, icons |

Keep red and green to small doses. If either is used as a section background, use a light tint (10–15% opacity) rather than the full solid color.

---

## Tints (for backgrounds, tags, and hover states)

| Color | 100% (solid) | Light tint (~15%) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Blue | `#1D5FA5` | `#E6F0F9` |
| Sunshine Yellow | `#F5B800` | `#FDF3D8` |
| Leaf Green | `#3E9B4F` | `#E5F3E7` |
| Cherry Red | `#D8342F` | `#FAE4E3` |

Light tints work well as: section backgrounds, tag/badge fills (paired with the solid color as the text), card backgrounds for house/team pages.

## Accessibility notes

- **Primary Blue on white** and **Charcoal Ink on white** both pass WCAG AA for body text.
- **Sunshine Yellow** has low contrast against white — never use it for text on a white background. Use it as a fill with Charcoal Ink or dark blue text on top, not as text itself.
- **Cherry Red on white** passes AA for text at normal sizes but is more comfortable used as an accent/fill rather than long paragraphs of red text.
- Always pair a colored background with a dark text color from the palette (Charcoal Ink or the darkest version of that color), never plain gray.

---

## House / team color idea

Since the uniforms already suggest a house system (red, green, blue, yellow), each class or team page on the site could use its house color as a light background tint and accent, while the main site frame (nav, footer, primary buttons) stays on Primary Blue and Off-White. This keeps the whole site consistent while still giving each house its own identity.

---

## Suggested pairing for typography

- **Headings:** a rounded, friendly sans-serif (e.g. Poppins, Quicksand, or Nunito) in Primary Blue or Charcoal Ink
- **Body text:** a clean, highly legible sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Open Sans, or Lato) in Charcoal Ink
- Avoid very thin font weights — young readers and parents scanning on mobile need clear, medium-weight text
